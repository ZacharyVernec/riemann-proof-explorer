Proof Spine, Bottlenecks & Critical Dependency Chain

This report analyzes the logical dependency DAG of the proof—tracing its mandatory mathematical bottlenecks (dominator tree rooted at Main.lean), its deepest critical dependency chain from the final RH/GRH theorems down to foundational algebraic-geometry leaves, and the key bridge modules connecting the major theories (interactive explorer in spine_dashboard.html).

Edge Orientation Convention: Throughout this report, a directed edge (u, v) means module u depends on v (u imports v). Hence Main.lean is the unique root/source at the apex of the proof, and foundational leaf modules sit at the base (height = 0).

Executive Overview: Mathematical Architecture of the Proof DAG

Two structural phenomena govern the logical organization of the proof:

  1. A Two-Branch Apex Junction (MainTheoremsAll): Right below Main.lean, MainTheoremsAll.lean unites two top-level proof branches:
    • The critical-line / RH & GRH theorem kernel (MainTheoremsCritical.lean $\to$ MainTheorems.lean $\to$ MainConclusion.lean), and
    • The explicit small-class-number Selmer bound over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ (CorGKOf.lean). Because both branches share the core automorphic-Galois and modularity-Selmer engines, major foundational bridges such as RowSrc.lean (strong local-global Galois compatibility) and TopMainZj.lean (automorphic/Galois/CM modularity inputs) attach directly beneath MainTheoremsAll in the dominator tree.
  2. A Deep Serial Algebraic-Geometric & Automorphic Spine: While high-level synthesis modules aggregate many parallel inputs, the deepest dependency chain in the proof forms a tight serial spine running from Main.lean through local-global Galois compatibility (RowSrc), weight-one geometric realization (GeometricRealizationOfWeightOne), finite-étale Kodaira-Spencer models (KsGoodHolds), Harris-Taylor correspondence extensions (FlagModelValueIndependent), Siegel family representability, dual abelian schemes, the Theorem of the Cube (TheoremOfCubeAbelianSchemeOverArbitraryBase), Iwasawa decomposition and unitary family existence (ExistsUnfFamily), Serre vanishing, and PEL Shimura toroidal compactifications (UniformisationConclusion) down to split algebraic tori (SplitTorusAff.lean).

    • CorGKOf.lean proves UpperTheory.V.SmallClassNumbers.corGK and bound over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$, pulling in ClassNumberThree, TopMainZj, DefiniteNHolds, and RowSrc.
    • MainTheorems.lean connects CritLine.lean, ClaimStrip.lean, and MainConclusion.lean to discharge allChi, rhOfAll, critAll, and claimAll.

2. Stratigraphic Layering Across Theories

The mathematical domains of the proof stratify naturally into five functional layers from the apex down to the base:

  1. Apex & Analytic Reflection Layer ((root), Supplements, Formalism/GammaFactorNonvanishing): Converts universal right half-plane nonvanishing (EveryCharacter) into the classical Riemann Hypothesis and Generalized Riemann Hypothesis via Euler-factor stripping (ClaimStrip) and functional-equation reflection (CritLine, RhOfRight).
  2. Upper Automorphic & Positivity Synthesis (PositivityTheory, upper Development): Constructs parallel weight-one Hilbert Eisenstein series, Rankin-Selberg zeta integrals on $\mathrm{GL}_3 \times \mathrm{GL}_2$ and $\mathrm{GL}_2 \times \mathrm{GL}_2$, local-global Galois representations, modularity-lifting patching reductions, and windowed Chebyshev character boxes.
  3. Core Geometric & Representation-Theoretic Engine (Development/AbelianVarietyTheory, Development/AutomorphicSpectrum, Development/WeilBound, CompanionTheory): Develops the theory of polarized abelian schemes, dual abelian varieties, de Rham and crystalline comparisons, Moriwaki arithmetic bigness, Harish-Chandra discrete series characters (DSInfCharCap), and Flath restricted tensor product factorizations.
  4. Specification & Harmonic-Analytic Infrastructure (Formalism): Provides the local-to-global theta-lift stage contracts (LinksStage), formal differential operator chart-independence (ThExprUniqOfChartIndep2), the master CM host / Hermitian lattice coordination structure (D7), local Bloch-Kato orthogonality (LocCoh), and local Tate duality (LocalTateDualityK).
  5. Algebraic-Geometric, Cohomological & Arakelov Bedrock (ReciprocityTheory, GroundTheory, Lemmata): Supplies PEL Shimura toroidal compactifications (UniformisationConclusion), uniqueness of Mumford semiabelian uniformisations (UnifUniqSt), the 24-law contract for polarized abelian schemes (GlobalSectionsRankAlgClosed), Arakelov Hermitian lattices and Bost-Zhang slope bounds (KernelsLattice), Whittaker multiplicity one (Injective), and Poitou-Tate nine-term Galois cohomology duality (PoitouTateNineTermGs).
