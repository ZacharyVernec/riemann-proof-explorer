Topical Hierarchy & Sub-Theory Organization

This document maps the topical organization of the mathematical proof across its major directories and sub-theories.

1. Top-Level Mathematical Domains

DomainMathematical Scope
Main.lean & Statements.leanStandalone target specifications and final proofs of the Riemann Hypothesis, right half-plane nonvanishing for Dirichlet $L$-functions, and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis.
SupplementsTop-level assembly kernels uniting the critical-line theorem track with the explicit small-class-number Selmer bound over $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$.
DevelopmentPrimary proof synthesis across abelian varieties, automorphic arithmetic, automorphic spectrum, the Weil bound & Moriwaki bigness, étale cohomology, discrete groups, and resolution of singularities.
FormalismAnalytic and algebraic specification contracts: local-to-global theta lifts, sharp restriction, period integrals, Hermitian curvature, $\mathrm{GL}_3$ mean squares, and local Bloch-Kato cohomology.
ReciprocityTheoryReductive groups, PEL Shimura toroidal compactifications, Mumford semiabelian uniformisations, $L^2$ $\bar\partial$-estimates, dual lattices, and semistable reduction.
GroundTheoryArakelov heights and theta functions, automorphic multiplicity one, profinite Poitou-Tate cohomology, global/local fields, $p$-adic Hodge theory, and arithmetic geometry.
CompanionTheorySpectral geometry, real reductive groups and Flath factorization, Harish-Chandra discrete series characters, and tempered local spectra.
PositivityTheoryAutomorphic positivity estimates, Rankin-Selberg $\mathrm{GL}_3 \times \mathrm{GL}_2$ and $\mathrm{GL}_2 \times \mathrm{GL}_2$ zeta integrals, and parallel weight-one Hilbert Eisenstein series.
LemmataStandalone linear-algebraic and matrix-determinant identities (such as Cauchy-Binet and dual Weyl pullbacks).

2. Core Sub-Theories (Depth 2 & Depth 3)

  • Development/AbelianVarietyTheory: Polarized abelian schemes, divisor and line-bundle proofs (DivisorProofs), inertia eigenvalues (InertiaEigenvalues), Iwasawa decompositions (IwasawaDecomposition), twisted operators (TwistedOperators), flag measures (FlagMeasures), Satake parameters (SatakeParameters), moduli and degeneration proofs, and the master catalog of literature specifications (PublishedTheorems).
  • Development/AutomorphicArithmetic: Windowed Chebyshev character boxes (CharacterBoxes), modularity-lifting threshold arguments and Kato's Euler system (AutomorphicVarieties), $\ell$-adic Galois representations and weight-one geometric realization (Foundations), unitary theta-correspondence pair lifts (ThetaCorrespondence), and Capelli differential operator identities (IntegralCasts).
  • Development/AutomorphicSpectrum: Block decomposition of smooth representations (BlockDecomposition), good root-tower and Arakelov volume theorems (GeometryProofs, DivisibilityProofs), and metric/Hermitian height comparisons (MetricProofs).
  • Development/WeilBound: Moriwaki arithmetic bigness and Hilbert-Samuel volume chains (CoreStatements/AutomorphicKernels/MoriwakiBigness), $p$-adic Hodge / étale cohomology comparisons, regularized Siegel-Weil and theta idempotents (RepresentationTheoryGL), and literature proof bridges (CoreStatements/PublishedTheorems).
  • Formalism/LocalToGlobal & Formalism/SharpRestriction: Indefinite and definite theta-lift stages, Siegel lattice theory, growth and cusp-span norm bounds, local Bloch-Kato finite-part cohomology (LevelLetterCocycles), and the windowed Landau nonvanishing criterion (TotientGaloisClasses).
  • ReciprocityTheory/ReductiveGroups: PEL-type Shimura toroidal compactifications (ToroidalCompactification), Mumford semiabelian degeneration and descent (ExtensionDescent), and split algebraic tori (SemiabelianSchemes).
  • GroundTheory/HeightsAndTheta: Arakelov Hermitian vector bundles, Bost-Zhang small-height slope inequalities (KernelsLattice), complex analytic sets, arithmetic Bézout intersections, and Hilbert-Samuel growth bounds.
