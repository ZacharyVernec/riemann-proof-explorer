## Cross-Cutting Ways the Lean Formalizations Differ from Published Literature

1. **Bespoke global CM host restrictions (`D7`, `Host E`, `HSp D`):** Dozens of Siegel–Weil, toroidal-compactification, trace-formula, and automorphic-form specs are **not** stated for general number fields or even arbitrary CM fields. `Host E` forces $E$ to contain $\sqrt{-d}$ for a prime $d \equiv 7 \pmod{16}$ with $E/E^+$ unramified everywhere, uniform archimedean signature $(p, q)$, and a unimodular alternating lattice; `D : D7` forces $E$ to be a specific Galois abelian CM field inside $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{Q_S q d})$, everywhere unramified over $E^+$, with `V : HSp D` requiring a globally unimodular self-dual $\mathcal{O}_E$-lattice.
2. **Pervasive domain/characteristic/prime restrictions:** Global $\mathrm{GL}_n$ automorphic theorems are mostly restricted to `[IsTotallyComplex E]` (or split into totally complex vs. totally real); $p$-adic $\mathrm{GL}_n$ and LLC theorems are restricted to `[CharZero k]` / completions of number fields; Green and depth-zero inertia theorems are restricted to prime fields $\mathbb{F}_g$ / $\mathbb{Q}_g$; several geometric/Shimura statements are restricted to characteristic $0$ or affine bases.
3. **Weakened conclusions and omitted equivalences:** Many categorical equivalences (prismatic Dieudonné, Bhatt–Scholze, Fontaine, Mumford degeneration, Casselman–Wallach, GAGA, Honda–Tate, Zarhin) are weakened to one-sided existence, drop essential surjectivity/full faithfulness/uniqueness/canonicality, or drop functoriality (e.g., `KLocalisation` and `KDwyerFriedlander` assert bare existence of *some* homomorphisms; `PoitouTate*` uses existential connecting maps; `NadelVanishingPair` omits identifying the ideal sheaf as $\mathcal{J}(X, \Delta)$).
4. **Rare strengthenings or missing axioms relative to literature:** A few specs are *stronger* than the cited source (e.g., `ShinGaloisRep` includes full monodromy compatibility away from $\ell$ without Caraiani's extra hypotheses; `CasselmanWallach` omits admissibility/finite-length on the Fréchet target `E`; `TaylorYoshidaSquareIntegrable` drops the imaginary quadratic subfield hypothesis), while `LocalConstantsExist` / `DeligneLocalConstants` **omit the monodromy-$N$ axiom** of Deligne (1973) §8.12 completely.
