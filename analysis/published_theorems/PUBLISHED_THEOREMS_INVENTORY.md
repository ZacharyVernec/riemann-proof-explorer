# Inventory of Existing Peer-Reviewed Theory Across `src/` (`community-9211277d`)

In this condensed readable edition (`src/`, **803 modules** reachable from `Main.lean`), existing peer-reviewed mathematics is separated cleanly at the directory boundary from the new proof argument:

1. **`Lib/` (495 modules, 61.6% of reachable codebase):** Background mathematical theories classed as known in the literature that Mathlib lacks, organized across **17** topical subsystems (`Lib/Adelic/`, `Lib/Shimura/`, `Lib/GaloisRep/`, `Lib/PadicHodge/`, `Lib/PadicComparison/`, `Lib/SiegelWeil/`, `Lib/Weil/`, etc.). Crucially, `Lib/` imports **zero** modules from `New/`.
2. **`New/` + apex (308 modules):** The setup, main reduction, derivatives, upper bound, second-moment spanning, determinant lower bound, one-lift comparisons, congruence/Selmer bounds, and discrete-series appendix.

This inventory catalogs the `Lib/` background subsystems against `INDEX.md` and `graph.sqlite` and documents how they collapse into Literature Supernodes in the [Full Lean Module DAG](../full_dag/FULL_DAG.md).
