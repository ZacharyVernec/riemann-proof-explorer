Mathematical Proof Blueprint: Riemann Hypothesis & Generalized Riemann Hypothesis

This Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) distills the formalized Lean proof's dependency structure into 45 key mathematical milestones arranged top-down across five stages (from Stage 0 final theorems at the top to Stage 4 foundational machinery at the bottom). Boxes represent major theorems, assembly bridges, or specification contracts anchored to concrete Lean declarations, collapsing internal helper modules between them. Wires point downward from a milestone to its direct prerequisite milestones (depends_on); reading bottom-up traces how foundational machinery combines to prove the main theorems. Click boxes to select one or more milestones, highlight their dependency chains, and reveal their cards below (click a selected box again to undo); click a box's target icon to jump directly to its card. Stages 0 and 1 present Lean-free natural-language explanations of each milestone's statement, assumptions, and proof strategy, with a toggle between short summaries and detailed explanations.
