Mathematical Blueprint: Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis & Generalized Riemann Hypothesis

Audience & Scope: This blueprint translates the formalization in lean/src into a self-contained, Lean-free mathematical exposition structured top-down across five stages (Stages 0–4). Every mathematical milestone links directly to its exact verified location in the source tree (file:// links) and matches blueprint_data.json.

Executive Overview: The Proof Strategy in Plain English

At the highest level, the proof establishes both the classical Riemann Hypothesis (RH) and the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis (GRH) for all Dirichlet $L$-functions by reducing critical-line zero localization ($\operatorname{Re}(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}$) to a single universal statement: no Dirichlet $L$-function $L(\chi, s)$ vanishes anywhere in the open right half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$ (away from the pole of $\zeta(s)$ at $s = 1$).

Once this right half-plane nonvanishing holds, the functional equations of $\zeta(s)$ and primitive Dirichlet $L$-functions (plus Euler-factor nonvanishing for $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 0$) mirror every zero with $\operatorname{Re}(s) < \tfrac{1}{2}$ onto a zero with $\operatorname{Re}(1 - s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$, forcing all nontrivial zeros onto $\operatorname{Re}(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}$.

To prove $L(\chi, s) \neq 0$ for $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$, the formalization executes a windowed Landau–Chebyshev–Galois strategy:

  1. Windowed Chebyshev-to-Zero-Free Principle (Analytic Engine): By a finite-level variant of Landau's nonnegativity theorem on cyclic character groups $\Lambda_\psi = \langle \psi \rangle$, if the weighted Chebyshev-type prime-power error sum $G_{\Lambda_\psi}(x)$ satisfies $$G_{\Lambda_\psi}(x) \le x^{\frac{1}{2} + O(\varepsilon)}$$ throughout sufficiently wide logarithmic windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$ ($\lambda > 1$) as $x \to \infty$ (for arbitrarily small $\varepsilon > 0$), then $L(\psi, s)$ cannot vanish in $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$.
  2. Galois-Theoretic Family Designs & Selmer/Shafarevich-Tate Growth Control: To build families of auxiliary primes and cyclic character twists that achieve these windowed Chebyshev cancellations without hitting a bottleneck from exceptional zeros or bad ramification, the proof couples explicit prime-pair sieve/design bounds with polynomial-in-log-conductor / subexponential upper bounds on Shafarevich-Tate / Bloch-Kato Selmer group lengths (VWin) across cyclotomic/abelian number fields.
  3. Modularity Lifting, $U(3)$ Selmer Counting & Kato's Euler System: Those Selmer growth bounds (VWin, and its explicit specialization to $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$) are proved via automorphy lifting, patching arguments, $U(3)$ Galois-cohomology counting over auxiliary imaginary quadratic fields $K^*(p)$, Poitou-Tate nine-term duality, weight-1 Hilbert Eisenstein series, and Kato's Euler system (ZetaUr).
  4. Local-Global Compatibility & Theta-Correspondence Lifts: The modularity and Selmer machinery requires strong local-global compatibility (Row0b) between $\ell$-adic Galois representations and polarized regular algebraic cuspidal automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n$, together with an explicit Local Langlands correspondence preserving tame/depth-zero types (recTExists). The formalization establishes Row0b via a weight-one geometric realization (crystalline towers, finite-étale Kodaira-Spencer models, and the holomorphic Lefschetz fixed-point formula) combined with global unitary theta-kernel Parseval lifts (TLiftK), and pairs this on the definite/indefinite theta-correspondence side with Siegel-Weil formulas, Harish-Chandra infinitesimal characters, and Flath factorization.
  5. Arakelov Geometry & Moriwaki Bigness (CovolX): Finally, controlling the rank, height, and lattice covolume constants of the arithmetic parameter families requires global Arakelov intersection lower bounds (CovolX, VolumeTheoremX), proved via PEL-type Shimura toroidal compactifications, Mumford semiabelian uniformisations, functorial resolution of singularities in characteristic zero, twisted étale cohomology finiteness, Bost-Zhang Hermitian lattice slope bounds, and Moriwaki's arithmetic bigness and Hilbert-Samuel volume theorems.

Specifically, the five top-level theorems proved in Main.lean assert:

  1. Every zero $s \in \mathbb{C}$ of $\zeta(s)$ other than the trivial negative even integers $s = -2(n+1)$ ($n \in \mathbb{N}$) and $s \neq 1$ satisfies $\operatorname{Re}(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}$.
  2. For every modulus $N \ge 1$, every Dirichlet character $\chi \pmod N$, and every $s \in \mathbb{C}$ with $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$ and ($\chi \neq 1$ or $s \neq 1$), $L(\chi, s) \neq 0$.
  3. For every $N \ge 1$, every character $\chi \pmod N$ (arbitrary or primitive), and every $s$ in the critical strip $0 < \operatorname{Re}(s) < 1$, $L(\chi, s) = 0 \implies \operatorname{Re}(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}$.

Stage 1: Functional-Equation & Character Reductions

Stage 1 bridges half-plane nonvanishing ($\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$) to critical-line zero localization ($0 < \operatorname{Re}(s) < 1 \implies \operatorname{Re}(s) = \tfrac{1}{2}$) by elementary complex analysis of Euler products, Gamma factors, and functional equations:

  • Critical-Line Assembly (MainTheoremsCritical.lean) and Top-Level Analytic Bridge (MainTheorems.lean) convert EveryCharacter into the four exported theorems (allChi, rhOfAll, critAll, claimAll).
  • Primitive Character Reflection (CritLine.lean): For any primitive character $\chi$, the archimedean factor $\gamma(\chi, s) \in \{\Gamma_{\mathbb{R}}(s), \Gamma_{\mathbb{R}}(s+1)\}$ has no zeros for $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 0$. Consequently, if $L(\chi, s) = 0$ with $0 < \operatorname{Re}(s) < \tfrac{1}{2}$, the completed $L$-function vanishes at $s$; by the functional equation relating $\Lambda(\chi, s)$ and $\Lambda(\chi^{-1}, 1-s)$, it follows that $L(\chi^{-1}, 1-s) = 0$ with $\operatorname{Re}(1 - s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$, contradicting half-plane nonvanishing.
  • Riemann Zeta Reflection (RhOfRight.lean): Deduces RiemannHypothesis directly from $\zeta(s) \neq 0$ on $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$ ($s \neq 1$) via the asymmetric functional equation $$\zeta(1 - s) = 2(2\pi)^{-s}\Gamma(s)\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi s}{2}\right)\zeta(s)$$ (checking explicitly that the cosine, Gamma, and $2(2\pi)^{-(1-s)}$ prefactors avoid all nontrivial zeros).
  • Imprimitive Character Reduction (ClaimStrip.lean, over HalfPlane.lean): Expresses $L(\chi, s)$ for an arbitrary character $\chi \pmod N$ of conductor $M \mid N$ as $$L(\chi, s) = L(\psi, s) \prod_{p \mid N} \bigl(1 - \psi(p)p^{-s}\bigr)$$ where $\psi$ is the primitive character inducing $\chi$. Because $|\psi(p)p^{-s}| \le p^{-\operatorname{Re}(s)} < 1$ whenever $\operatorname{Re}(s) > 0$, each Euler factor is nonzero in the critical strip, transferring any zero of $L(\chi, s)$ to $L(\psi, s)$ and then reflecting across $s \mapsto 1 - s$.

Stage 2: Main Bridge Theorems & Dominators

Stage 2 contains the top assembly bottlenecks of the proof DAG where the four major pillars—local-global Galois representations, Selmer/modularity window bounds, theta-correspondence lifts, and Arakelov covolume estimates—converge to prove EveryCharacter.

  1. Master Synthesis (UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter) — MainConclusion.lean: Proves half-plane nonvanishing unconditionally by instantiating OpenLiteratureStatements with (galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter, recTExists, galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter) and applying vwin_of_row to galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter to obtain VWin, feeding both into every_character_of_literature_of_vWin.
  2. Covolume & Analytic Wrapper (RiemannHypothesisOfEveryCharacter) — RiemannHypothesisOfEveryCharacter.lean: Synthesizes operator bounds, Arakelov covolumes, and local matching, linking EveryCharacter and CovolumeOfSlimAndHyp.
  3. Character-Box Window Assembly (plugAll) — EveryCharacter.lean: Derives EveryCharacter from the Green prime-character property, VWin, and the family-design theorem TopShape by invoking zeroFreeAll_of_topG ($\lambda = 3/2$) and lifting primitive nonvanishing to imprimitive characters.
  4. Theta-Correspondence & Covolume Synthesis (every_character_of_literature_of_vWin) — EveryCharacterOfLiteratureOfVWin.lean: Ties together over 70 proved theorems (PositivityTheory, CompanionTheory, GroundTheory, Development) into definite-side theta-lift hypotheses (SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean), Arakelov covolumes (topCovolSlim2_use), and VWin to prove EveryCharacter.
  5. Strong Local-Global Galois Compatibility (RowSrc) — RowSrc.lean: Feeds TLiftK (unitary theta-kernel Parseval lift) and GeoOne (weight-one geometric realization) through GaloisRepresentationsOfThetaLift to prove Row0b recT: existence of compatible $\ell$-adic Galois representations attached to regular algebraic polarized cuspidal automorphic forms on $\mathrm{GL}_n$, matching Frobenius characteristic polynomials with Satake parameters away from $\ell$ and satisfying potentially semistable Weil-Deligne compatibility above $\ell$.
  6. Modularity-Selmer Window Bridge (VwinOfRow → TopMainZj → WindowBoundOfLiteratureLevel):
    • VwinOfRow.lean turns Row0b recT and baseChangeN into VWin.
    • TopMainZj.lean packages proved local $\mathrm{GL}_n$, automorphic, Galois-cohomological, and CM abelian-variety theorems into LiteratureRankNHypothesis.
    • WindowBoundOfLiteratureLevel.lean executes patching, descent-vanishing reductions, Kato's bounds, and unitary $U(3)$ Selmer counting to produce VWin.
  7. Arakelov Covolume Bridges (TopCovolSlim2Use & CovolumeOfSlimAndHyp):
    • TopCovolSlim2Use.lean turns resolution, Moriwaki bigness, Fargues exactness, Zarhin, Nef Riemann-Roch, and Scholze-Weinstein into CovolX.
    • CovolumeOfSlimAndHyp.lean turns Slim and HypothesesXH into CovolX via Moret-Bailly second jets and Lie-slope height estimates (CovolumeOfHeightHyp).
  8. Small-Class-Number Selmer Growth (CorGKOf) — CorGKOf.lean: Specializes the unitary $U(3)$ Selmer bound unconditionally to $K^*(3) = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ (class number $1$), proving the explicit polynomial growth bound $\sum_{l \in L_s} \log(l)\,\mathrm{len}(\mathrm{Sha}_U(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}^{-e})) / e_{\mathcal{O}} \le C(e+1)^A$.

Stage 3: Major Theory Pillars

Stage 3 comprises the seven self-contained mathematical engines supplying Stage 2.

3.1 Analytic Explicit Formula & Windowed Landau Criterion

  • Design Choices to Tied Chebyshev Inequality (DesWin.lean): Given admissible prime-pair designs (DesignExists) and closed estimates for ramification, rank, divisibility (LinkDIVt), spectral gap (LinkG), Schur norms (LinkS), and global consistency (LinkGLOBAL), produces connected parameter families (FamP, defined over ParConsts) obeying the tied Chebyshev inequality on scale windows.
  • Windowed Landau Nonvanishing (TopWin.lean): Using the weighted Cesàro prime-power sum $$G_\Lambda(y) = G_{\zeta}(y) + \sum_{\psi \in \Lambda \setminus \{1\}} w(\psi)\,\operatorname{Re}\bigl(g_\psi(y)\bigr)$$ (where $g_\chi(y) = \sum_{n \le y} \Lambda(n)\chi(n)(y/n - 1) + y \frac{L'(\chi, 1)}{L(\chi, 1)}$), proves zeroFreeOn_of_tiedWin_cyc: boundedness $G_{\Lambda_\psi}(x) \le x^{1/2 + C\varepsilon}$ across arbitrarily large windows $[Y, Y^\lambda]$ ($\lambda > 1$) forces $L(\psi, s) \neq 0$ for $\operatorname{Re}(s) > \tfrac{1}{2}$ via Landau's abscissa theorem.

3.2 Galois Representations, Crystalline Realization & Lefschetz Traces

  • Weight-One Geometric Realization (GeometricRealizationOfWeightOne.lean): Constructs GeoOne by synthesizing crystalline towers, $p$-adic Witt lattices, finite-étale Kodaira-Spencer covers (KsGoodHolds), and Hodge-Tate jumps.
  • Holomorphic Lefschetz Fixed-Point Formula (LefschetzFixedPointFiniteEtaleCorrespondence.lean): Proves that for finite étale correspondences $c_1, c_2 : Y \to Z$ on smooth projective $\mathbb{C}$-schemes with isolated fixed points $F$ and tangent differentials $A_y$ ($\det(I - A_y) \neq 0$), $$\sum_{b=0}^d (-1)^b \operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}_{c_1} \circ c_2^* \mid H^b(Z, \Omega^a_{Z/\mathbb{C}})\bigr) = \sum_{y \in F} \frac{[X^a] \det(I + X \cdot A_y)}{\det(I - A_y)}.$$

3.3 Theta-Kernel Parseval Lifts & Hilbert Eisenstein Series

  • Unitary Theta-Kernel Lift (TPGHolds.lean): Proves tLift_holds_k (TLiftK): regular algebraic cuspidal representations of $U(n)$ lift nonvanishingly to definite/skew unitary spaces $U(d)$ for sufficiently large $d$, via standard Siegel-Weil formulas, Eisenstein continuation, doubling zeta integrals, and Schwartz-Bruhat test vectors.
  • Weight-One Hilbert Eisenstein Series (HilbertEisensteinWeightOneHolds.lean): Proves existence, moderate growth, smoothness, $K$-finiteness, and Fourier expansions of parallel weight-1 Hilbert Eisenstein series used to build congruences and raise levels.

3.4 Local Langlands with Tame Types & Archimedean/Adelic Representation Theory

  • Local Langlands Correspondence (recTExists) (RecTExists.lean): Constructs rc : IrrGL(E_w) → WD(W_{E_w}) satisfying LawsT (dimension, local Artin map, Zelevinsky segment sums, twists, central characters, contragredients, injectivity on good representations, and depth-zero tame inertia compatibility).
  • Capelli Infinitesimal Character of Discrete Series (DiscreteSeriesInfinitesimalCharacter.lean): Proves DSInfCharCap for smooth vectors of discrete series representations of $U(p,q)$ using Casselman-Osborne Lie algebra cohomology, Koszul resolutions, and Verma covers.
  • Unitary Admissibility & Flath Factorization (FinalB.lean): Proves admissibility of irreducible unitary representations and restricted tensor product decomposition $\pi \cong \bigotimes'_v \pi_v$ over all places of $F$.

3.5 Unitary $U(3)$ Selmer Growth & Kato's Euler System

  • $U(3)$ Selmer Growth Bound (CountI) (CountI.lean): Combines the global $U(3)$ Selmer bound CorGK over quadratic CM fields $K^*(p)$ with cyclic subgroup orbit sums (orbSum) to bound $\sum_{l \in L_s} \log(l) \sum_\psi V_n(\psi)$ polynomially in $(N \cdot t)^A$.
  • Kato Euler System Integrality (ZetaUr) (ZetaUr.lean): Proves GlobalTheory.Ord2.ZetaUr from Kato's Euler-system integrality and the smoothed zeta-class Hodge-Tate law.

3.6 Good Root Towers & Moriwaki Arithmetic Bigness

  • Good Root-Tower & Volume Theorem (GoodTowerVolumeTheoremAlt.lean): Proves GoodTowerExistsX ∧ VolumeTheoremX by combining Arakelov intersection systems, Moriwaki Hilbert-Samuel/bigness, Fargues Hodge-Tate exactness, Zarhin's trick, Bost-Zhang small-height lattice vectors, and complex theta uniformisation.
  • Moriwaki Arithmetic Bigness (Outright.lean): Establishes MoriwakiBigStatement and HSUse unconditionally from characteristic-zero resolution of singularities, Poincaré-Lelong arithmetic intersection systems, and dense bounded-height curves.

Stage 4: Foundational Machinery

Stage 4 provides the reusable arithmetic-geometric, cohomological, and analytic bedrock imported across Stages 2 and 3.

4.1 Abelian Schemes & Shimura Toroidal Compactifications (ReciprocityTheory)

  • PEL Shimura Toroidal Compactification (PartI) — UniformisationConclusion.lean: Defines integral toroidal models with split boundary fibers, Mumford semiabelian uniformisation Model.N3 (PELUnif), torsion/Weil-pairing identification, and formal power-series arc lifts Model.N4.
  • Canonical Uniqueness of Mumford Uniformisation (UnifUniqSt) — UnifUniqSt.lean: Guarantees uniqueness up to canonical isomorphism of degeneration data sharing a polarized semiabelian scheme and rigidified line bundle.
  • 24-Law Contract for Polarized Abelian Schemes (Laws) — GlobalSectionsRankAlgClosed.lean: Encapsulates Riemann-Roch ($\dim H^0(A, L^n) = n^g$), base change, ampleness ($n \ge 4$), Theorem of the Cube and Square, rigidity, Weil pairing nondegeneracy, theta-group irreducibility, Serre's torsion rigidity ($M \ge 3$), and finite-flat quotients.
  • Split Tori & Torsion Subgroup Schemes (splitTorusAff) — SplitTorusAff.lean: The terminal foundational leaf of the longest critical dependency chain, defining split tori $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathcal{O}_S[X])$, $\mathbb{G}_m$, and $N$-torsion schemes.

4.2 Étale Cohomology & Resolution of Singularities (Development)

  • Finiteness of Twisted Étale Cohomology (goal_of) — H0TwistOverClosedField.lean: Proves finiteness of $H^n_{\text{ét}}(X, \mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z}(i))$ for proper schemes over fields ($q$ invertible) via double induction on degree $n$ and Krull dimension, alterations, curve Kummer theory, and ghost-killing.
  • Functorial Resolution in Characteristic Zero — ResolutionCharZero.lean: Discharges ResolutionCharZero using FunctorialResolution.functorialResolutionCharZero (embedded blowups along smooth centers with strict normal crossings divisors functorial under smooth morphisms and field extensions).

4.3 Global Arithmetic, Cohomology & Harmonic Analysis (GroundTheory & DiscreteGroups)

  • Poitou-Tate Nine-Term Duality — PoitouTateNineTermGs.lean: Proves poitouTate_duality_gs, finiteness and perfect pairing of $\mathrm{Sha}^1$ and $\mathrm{Sha}^2$ (poitouTate_sha_gs), and exactness of the full nine-term Poitou-Tate sequence (poitouTate_nineTerm_gs) for Galois groups $G_{K,S}$ unramified outside $S$.
  • Whittaker Multiplicity One on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ — Injective.lean: Proves that any smooth left-invariant cuspidal automorphic form on $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_K)$ whose Whittaker function vanishes identically is identically zero, via Fourier induction along unipotent rows.
  • Arakelov Hermitian Lattices & Bost-Zhang Slope Bound — KernelsLattice.lean: Defines HermLattice, Arakelov subDegree, quotDegree, slope, and LineOfLargeDegree.Statement (existence of a nonzero small-height vector over an extension $K'/K$ with $\operatorname{lineDegree}(v) \ge \mu(F) - \frac{1}{2}\log(i) - \varepsilon$).
  • Rapid Decay of Compactly Supported Cusp-Form Twists — PiVecExHolds.lean: Proves that twisting a smooth cusp form invariant under the split center by a compactly supported continuous function of $\log|\det|$ yields an $L^2(H_n, \nu)$ vector with finite moments against every power of the height weight.

4.4 Formalism Specification Contracts (Formalism)

  • Local Bloch-Kato Orthogonality & Tate Duality (LocCoh) — LocCoh.lean: Defines continuous $p$-adic Galois representations GRep, IsDR, and exact cup-product orthogonality (bkACore, bkBCore) of finite-part cocycles $H^1_f$.
  • Master Arithmetic-Automorphic Coordination Package (D7) — RhoDetPrimeEqMuTildeMulChiPow.lean: Coordinates self-dual Hermitian lattices, CM hosts, column chains, relative Hermitian determinants, Fontaine period rings (LocTied), Galois types, and theta jets across the analytic and geometric bridges.

How Literature & Hypothesis Bundles Are Discharged

To understand what is unconditionally proved versus parameterized in intermediate modules:

  • Despite directory and structure names such as PublishedTheorems, PublishedInputs, LiteratureTheorems, OpenLiteratureStatements, OpenImageCondition, and LiteratureRankNHypothesis, direct inspection of the source confirms that these are NOT unproved axioms or uninstantiated assumptions in the final theorems:
    • In MainConclusion.lean, UpperTheory.FinalTheorems.everyCharacter takes zero external hypothesis arguments and invokes everyCharacter_of_row UpperTheory.Gal.Int.galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter.
    • Inside everyCharacter_of_row (MainConclusion.lean), OpenLiteratureStatements (which has 3 fields: caraianiLocalGlobalDual, localLanglandsExists, galoisRepOfPolarizedPairLit) is unconditionally discharged by ⟨h, UpperTheory.Zero.LLC.recTExists, h⟩.
    • Likewise, OpenImageCondition (which has 1 field: galoisRepOfPolarizedPairLit) is unconditionally discharged in VwinOfRow.lean by OpenImageCondition.mk h.
    • All other results packaged inside EveryCharacterOfLiteratureOfVWin.lean and TopMainZj.lean (including Moriwaki bigness, characteristic-zero resolution, Poitou-Tate duality, global Artin reciprocity, Bernstein-Zelevinsky finiteness, Jacquet-Shalika rigidity, Casselman-Wallach, Clozel purity, unitary Siegel-Weil, and Grothendieck deformation) are proved theorems in PositivityTheory, CompanionTheory, Development, and GroundTheory that merely accept a dummy O parameter for modular wiring.
    • Similarly, Formalism structures (D7, LocCoh, FamP) and ReciprocityTheory specifications (PartI, UnifUniqSt, Laws) serve as abstract mathematical interface contracts (Prop definitions / structures) whose concrete obligations are fulfilled by proved theorems along the proof chain.
